How does Sage Intacct handle manufacturing overhead allocation? ✕
Sage Intacct uses dimensional reporting to allocate overhead costs across products, departments, locations, and custom segments. You can set up allocation rules that automatically distribute costs based on direct labor, machine hours, or other drivers. This gives you accurate product costing and profitability analysis without manual spreadsheets.
