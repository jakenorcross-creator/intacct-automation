What's inside

Most 501(c)(6) associations manage complex, multi-stream revenue across dues, events, certifications, and sponsorships - yet many still rely on financial systems that were never designed for the job. AMS-to-finance reconciliation consumes 40 or more hours every month. Event revenues swing 35-50% based on registration changes. And compliance risks, from proxy tax miscalculations to late Form 990 filings, are growing harder to manage manually.

This report reveals where the gaps are widest and what leading associations are doing differently. You will see why 92% of CFOs at membership organizations struggle with forecast accuracy when attendance shifts, why only 52% of associations are audit-ready without outside support, and how manual inter-entity allocations consume 25 or more hours monthly across the 5-13 entities a typical mid-market association manages. The findings make clear that the gap between modernized and legacy finance operations is widening.

Sage Intacct is built to address exactly these challenges - with multi-entity consolidation, dimensional reporting by revenue stream, and audit-ready controls designed for nonprofit compliance. This report shows you what good looks like and where to start.
