About this webinar

Payroll complexity rarely arrives all at once. It builds - an extra entity here, a manual reconciliation there - until the workarounds you put in place start to feel like the process itself. When that happens, the risk is real, but it can be hard to say exactly where it lives.

This session is designed to help you find it. We will look at how disconnected HR, time, and expenses data creates gaps in accuracy, and how unifying those sources removes the need for parallel checks and re-keyed figures.

Sage has run payroll for 30 years, supporting organisations with genuine complexity to manage. In this webinar, we will show you how Sage People helps teams achieve strong accuracy on high-volume pay runs, give your people secure cloud access to run multi-brand payroll from anywhere, and build a clearer case for leadership by quantifying time saved, errors reduced, and compliance made simpler.

What you'll learn

  • How to unify HR, time, and expenses into a single source of truth - so complex, high-volume pay runs are accurate without extra headcount or manual checks
  • How to quantify the real cost of payroll risk and present a clear ROI case to leadership, covering time saved, errors reduced, and compliance simplified

If payroll complexity feels like a risk you manage rather than a process you control, join us - and see how that can change.
