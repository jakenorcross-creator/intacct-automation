FAQs

Accordion Panel Title 1 What is social housing and how is it funded?
Content Spot 8
Social housing provides affordable rented or shared ownership homes, often delivered by local authorities, registered providers, or housing associations. Funding comes from a mix of rent income, government grants, capital investment, and sometimes loans. Social housing funds are closely scrutinised and must follow strict rules.
Accordion Panel Title 2 How does Sage Intacct help manage social housing investment?
Content Spot 9
Sage Intacct helps you track investment into social housing projects—by scheme, asset, or region. Capture spend and returns from each social housing investment, allocate costs accurately, and report to investors or boards with audit-ready detail.
Accordion Panel Title 3 Can Sage Intacct support the social housing decarbonisation fund?
Content Spot 10
Yes. Sage Intacct lets you tag and monitor decarbonisation-related spend and outcomes. Track funding from the social housing decarbonisation fund, allocate it correctly, and show stakeholders your progress towards targets.
