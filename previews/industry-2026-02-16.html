FAQs

Accordion Panel Title 1 What is social housing and how is it funded?
Content Spot 8
Social housing offers affordable housing for people with low incomes or special needs. Funding comes from a mix of public grants, local authority support, and rent income. Sage Intacct helps social housing providers track each funding source, ensure proper allocation, and produce clear, compliant accounts.
Accordion Panel Title 2 What is the difference between social housing and council housing?
Content Spot 9
Council housing is owned and managed by local authorities. Social housing can also be managed by housing associations or charities. Both require robust financial controls. Sage Intacct supports a variety of funding streams, regulatory demands, and management models found in social and council housing finance.
Accordion Panel Title 3 How does Sage Intacct help with regulator of social housing compliance?
Content Spot 10
Sage Intacct provides audit-ready controls, automated workflows, and dimensional reporting tailored to sector regulations. This helps finance teams meet the exacting requirements of the regulator of social housing, with built-in documentation to support compliance reviews and external audits.
Accordion Panel Title 4 Can social services help with housing funding?
Content Spot 11
Social services may provide grants or financial support to individuals or organisations. Sage Intacct allows you to track these funds, allocate them to projects or clients, and report on their use, supporting your statutory and regulatory accounting needs.
Accordion Panel Title 5 Does Sage Intacct support social housing investment reporting?
Content Spot 12
Yes. Track investment in housing stock, capital repairs, renovations, and sustainability projects. Dimensional reporting and real-time dashboards show the financial impact of each investment, helping organisations justify spend and attract future funding.
