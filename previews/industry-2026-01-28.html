FAQs

Accordion Panel Title 1 What are mega churches and how do they handle finances?
Content Spot 8
Mega churches are large congregations—often thousands or more—requiring robust financial frameworks. They manage multiple entities, campuses, and a mix of income sources like donations, rentals, and cafes. Sage Intacct centralizes these for better transparency and stewardship.
Accordion Panel Title 2 Do mega churches pay taxes in the US?
Content Spot 9
US mega churches, as nonprofit religious organisations, typically do not pay federal income tax. Still, they must comply with IRS guidelines and file specific financial documents. Sage Intacct supports tracking and documentation for compliance.
Accordion Panel Title 3 How much money do mega churches make?
Content Spot 10
Mega churches manage from hundreds of thousands to millions in donations, grants, and earned income each year. Sage Intacct offers real-time visibility into revenue streams, helping you analyse trends and make informed, responsible decisions.
Accordion Panel Title 4 What denomination are mega churches?
Content Spot 11
Mega churches span many denominations—Baptist, nondenominational, Methodist, Pentecostal, and others. Sage Intacct adapts to the unique financial structures and reporting needs of each denomination, supporting various governance models.
Accordion Panel Title 5 How can Sage Intacct help multi-campus churches?
Content Spot 12
Sage Intacct helps multi-campus churches consolidate accounts, automate inter-campus transactions, and report by location or ministry. You gain an accurate, complete view of your entire ministry’s finances.
Accordion Panel Title 6 How does Sage Intacct handle donor restrictions?
Content Spot 13
You can assign and track restrictions for every donation or grant. Sage Intacct keeps restricted, temporarily restricted, and unrestricted funds separated, automating compliance with donor intent and reporting.
Accordion Panel Title 7 What reporting tools are available for board oversight?
Content Spot 14
Sage Intacct’s dashboards and financial statements can be customised for boards and committees. You can surface key information for oversight—giving, spending, assets—while maintaining drill-down access for finance teams.
Accordion Panel Title 8 Can Sage Intacct help track grants and funds?
Content Spot 15
Yes, Sage Intacct tracks all types of grants and funds—by source, restriction, or program. Automated workflows and dimensional reporting make it easy to satisfy grantor requirements and prepare for audits.
Accordion Panel Title 9 How does Sage Intacct support IRS compliance?
Content Spot 16
With built-in nonprofit controls, Sage Intacct helps you maintain records that satisfy IRS and denominational audit standards. Automated workflows support documentation for tax-exempt status and annual reporting.
Accordion Panel Title 10 Can Sage Intacct manage property and facility finances?
Content Spot 17
Yes, manage facility budgets, capital projects, and asset lifecycles in a single place. Track purchases, depreciation, and even financial impacts of campuses up for sale, helping you plan for major property decisions.
Accordion Panel Title 11 Why are mega churches so popular, and how does that affect finances?
Content Spot 18
Popularity brings diverse contributions and complex financial needs. As congregations grow, strong processes and audit-ready controls help manage increased donations, expansion projects, and multi-campus operations.
Accordion Panel Title 12 What are mega churches categorized as for accounting?
Content Spot 19
Mega churches are typically classified as nonprofit religious organisations for accounting. This drives specific reporting, donor tracking, and compliance standards—functions Sage Intacct supports out-of-the-box.
