FAQs

Accordion Panel Title 1 How does Sage Intacct help manage council housing finances?
Content Spot 8
Sage Intacct centralises financial management for council housing by automating accounts, simplifying reporting, and providing real-time insights. This helps track spending, manage subsidies, and meet regulatory requirements with less manual work.
Accordion Panel Title 2 Can Sage Intacct support housing benefit calculations?
Content Spot 9
You gain fast, detailed analysis for housing benefit calculations by tracking rent, subsidies, and tenant data across properties. Dimensional breakdowns support accurate eligibility and ensure funding is allocated correctly.
Accordion Panel Title 3 How does Sage Intacct address housing disrepair costs?
Content Spot 10
You can monitor and report on housing disrepair expenses at the property, scheme, or fund level. Automated workflows ensure costs are tracked, approvals are managed, and spending aligns with your compliance and funding policies.
Accordion Panel Title 4 Is it possible to consolidate multiple housing entities?
Content Spot 11
Multi-entity consolidation combines results from different associations, trusts, or branches. You can report on individual units or overall position, with no need for spreadsheet-based workarounds.
Accordion Panel Title 5 How are audits and compliance managed for housing projects?
Content Spot 12
Sage Intacct maintains full audit trails for each transaction, grant, and payment. It helps meet housing executive and regulator standards and is always ready for external audit or inspection.
Accordion Panel Title 6 Can I track affordable housing grant funds?
Content Spot 13
Yes, Sage Intacct enables you to track each grant by source, property, or use. Ensure funds for affordable housing are spent correctly, with clear reporting to satisfy stakeholders.
Accordion Panel Title 7 How does Sage Intacct help during a housing market crash?
Content Spot 14
During market downturns, you get up-to-date forecasts and scenario planning. This helps you understand risk, optimise budgets, and identify where to prioritise support for tenants or repairs.
Accordion Panel Title 8 What role do dashboards play for housing executives?
Content Spot 15
Dashboards show executives tailored views of arrears, maintenance costs, housing market trends, and project progress. This supports better governance and faster decisions across every level.
Accordion Panel Title 9 Are role-based permissions available for housing officers?
Content Spot 16
Yes, housing officers only access the data relevant to their responsibilities. Role-based permissions enable secure sharing of tenant arrears, budget, and repairs without exposing other data.
Accordion Panel Title 10 How does Sage Intacct support project accounting for social housing?
Content Spot 17
You can manage individual social housing projects, track funding, monitor costs, and produce detailed financial reports for internal and external stakeholders—all within the same system.
Accordion Panel Title 11 Can I track repairs and maintenance costs by property?
Content Spot 18
Yes, Sage Intacct lets you track every cost for housing repairs, maintenance, and disrepair by property, scheme, or contractor. Gain clearer insights for budgeting and compliance.
Accordion Panel Title 12 How does Sage Intacct handle affordable housing requirements?
Content Spot 19
Sage Intacct supports the financial management of affordable housing schemes by tracking funding, monitoring spend, and providing transparent reporting for councils, regulators, and housing associations.
