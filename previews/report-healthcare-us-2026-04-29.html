What you'll discover

Healthcare CFOs face mounting pressure from complex multi-entity consolidation across hospital networks, physician groups, and affiliated entities. Manual processes and spreadsheet dependency create audit risk while delaying critical decisions. This research study analyzes survey responses from 400 healthcare finance leaders to reveal the real impact of financial automation on close performance. You'll see concrete data on how organizations using automated intercompany eliminations achieve 2.5X faster closes compared to manual processes. The study also quantifies the widespread reliance on spreadsheets despite known risks and shows how real-time visibility reduces the time from close to board-ready insights by 11 days per quarter.
