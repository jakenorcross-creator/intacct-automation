What's inside

If your sales, production, inventory, and finance teams each work from different data, you are not running one business - you are running four. UK discrete manufacturers face a compounding problem: siloed systems create planning errors that cascade into missed deliveries, inaccurate costs, and cash flow surprises that only become visible weeks too late.

This eBook reveals why visibility gaps are not a people or process problem - they are a data connectivity problem. You will learn how inventory inaccuracies extend beyond the warehouse to work centres, unrecorded scrap, and part-built assemblies. You will see how the disconnect between operations and finance causes manufacturers to underprice jobs or lose bids entirely, and how unreliable operational data makes cash flow forecasting little more than guesswork.

The eBook sets out how connecting planning, production, inventory, sales, and finance on a single platform - like Sage X3 - gives you one accurate version of the truth. That means confident delivery commitments, real-time margin protection, and a cash position you can actually plan around.

Content Spot 1

Sage X3 connects every stage of the plan-to-cash cycle in one platform - so your teams always work from the same data, and you always know where margin, stock, and cash actually stand.
